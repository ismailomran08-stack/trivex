The model

One coordinated contract. Regional execution.

Trivex operates as a Burlington-based prime contractor with a coordinated network of vetted regional partners. Every project sits under a single Trivex contract, single point of accountability, and a disciplined trade-sequencing playbook — with execution carried by partners who already know their local code, inspectors, and labour pool.

For franchisors and multi-unit operators, the practical advantage is repeatability. The standards that get a build approved in Burlington translate cleanly to Fredericton, Bridgewater, or Upper Tantallon — without losing speed or quality on rollout number five, ten, or twenty.

Single prime contract. Local execution. Engineer-of-record coordination, municipal permit and inspection management, and brand-standard compliance delivered turnkey across every market we operate in.
